# H&M Retail Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on retail sales analysis through an interactive Excel dashboard inspired by H&M's fashion retail business.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance, revenue, profitability, product categories, customer segments, regional performance, and sales channels. It transforms transaction-level data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

**Project Type:** Retail Sales Data Analysis
**Tool:** Microsoft Excel
**Domain:** Fashion & Retail Analytics
**Dataset Size:** 1,500 transaction records
**Project Format:** Excel Dashboard

---

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall sales revenue and profitability.
* Identify the highest-performing product categories.
* Compare revenue across different regions.
* Evaluate online and in-store sales performance.
* Understand purchasing patterns among new and returning customers.
* Analyze monthly revenue trends.
* Identify products contributing significantly to revenue.
* Monitor discounts, customer ratings, and inventory status.

## Key Insights

Based on the workbook's existing KPI report and pivot analysis:

### 1. Women Is the Leading Revenue Category

The Women category generates 2,944,471 in revenue, making it the highest-revenue category in the dataset.

### 2. The West Leads Regional Revenue

The West region generates 2,827,307 in revenue, followed by the South region at 2,397,907.

### 3. Online Sales Have a Slight Lead

Online sales contribute 3,655,457 in revenue, compared with 3,559,271 from in-store sales.

### 4. New and Returning Customers Contribute Similar Revenue

Returning customers generate 3,613,655, while new customers generate 3,601,073. Revenue is therefore distributed almost equally between these two customer groups.

### 5. Overall Profitability

The dataset records total profit of 1,488,817 and a reported profit margin of 20.64%.

*These findings describe the supplied dataset and should not be interpreted as verified performance figures published by H&M.*

## Business Recommendations

Based on the observed patterns, the following actions could be explored:

* **Category Strategy:** Investigate the factors driving Women's category performance and identify opportunities in lower-revenue categories.
* **Regional Strategy:** Examine the sales drivers in the West and South regions to identify effective practices that could be applied elsewhere.
* **Channel Optimization:** Compare customer behavior, discounts, and profitability across online and in-store channels before allocating additional resources.
* **Customer Retention:** Develop targeted loyalty initiatives and compare repeat-purchase behavior over time.
* **Product Optimization:** Evaluate product-level revenue, margins, and inventory availability to improve merchandising decisions.
* **Discount Management:** Assess whether discount levels improve sales volume sufficiently to justify their effect on profitability.

These recommendations are proposed actions, not outcomes measured by the current analysis.
